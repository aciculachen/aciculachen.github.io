Kevin M. Chen

PhD Student, OSU CSE

Hello, my name is Kevin Michael Chen, also known as "Acicula" Chen, or Chen Chi-Wen (hanzi:陳啟文). I am currently a Ph.D. student in Computer Science and Engineering at The Ohio State University, advised by Dr.Anish Arora.

My research interests are driven by a fundamental question: How do learned models work internally to produce the behaviors we observe? This curiosity has led me to focus on mechanistic interpretability, with particular interests in physical world models and cross-modal generative AI.

Apart from the academic activity, I am an amateur guitar player having more than 10 years of self-practicing guitar. The guitar hero who inspires and influences me the most is American singer-songwriter John Mayer. I study heavily on his early acoustic songs, like his debut EP Inside Wants Out and the first studio album Room for Squares. Also, I am proud of having his signature Martin guitar, the Martin OM28 JM. Check out here for my JM covers.

I was born in Seattle and spent most of my life in Taiwan. Now, I've returned to the U.S. to pursue my Ph.D.

Guitars Collection
2021-
Martin OM-28 John Mayer

This guitar was luckily found and purchased through a friend (also a big fan of John Mayer) working in a guitar shop in Taipei, Taiwan. The guitar is the first official John Mayer Signature model made by Martin and was produced as a limited run of only 404, and I own the #173. The guitar features a solid Engelmann spruce top and solid East Indian rosewood back and sides.
